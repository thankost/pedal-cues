Everything a backing-track rig needs

Tested in Reaper; works in any DAW that can drag in MIDI clips and send a MIDI track to your pedals.

Presets & scenes

Your Quad Cortex or QC Mini presets, scene names and colours as tiles (on the Mini, footswitch Pages I and II). Sync your setlists, preset names, scene names and colours straight from the QC over USB. Scenes laid out like the pedal: A–D on top, E–H below. Scenes and stomps load their own preset first, or act on the one already loaded.

Preset names without typing

Sync from the Quad Cortex over USB, import a Helix / HX setlist or bundle from HX Edit (preset and snapshot names and colours), or read the names over MIDI from Strymon TimeLine / BigSky / Mobius, Boss DD / RV / MD-500, GT-1000 and Fractal Axe-Fx III / FM9 / FM3 / VP4 (beta, read-only).

Stomps, looper, tuner, gig view

Footswitch on/off, the Looper X (record, overdub, play, undo, reverse, half speed), tuner for guitar changes, tap tempo, Gig View on/off, and Preset / Scene / Stomp mode, each one drag away.

Expression automation

Volume swells, fades, wah rhythms or a delay mix that opens into the chorus: anything you assign to Expression 1 or 2 on the QC, tempo-synced. No pedal needed.

Kemper Profiler & Player

Pick Kemper on the first tab: performances with their five slots, effect modules A–D, X, MOD, delay and reverb (with tails), tuner, tap tempo, morph and rotary, plus wah, pitch, volume and morph pedal moves. No physical pedal needed.

Fractal, Line 6, HeadRush, Darkglass and any MIDI device (beta)

Pages for Helix, HX Stomp, HX Effects, POD Go, Helix Stadium, Axe-Fx II, AX8, FX8, HeadRush (Core, Prime, Flex Prime, Pedalboard, Gigboard, MX5), Darkglass Anagram, Infinity 500 Combo and Exponent 500, and Neural DSP Nano Cortex, all from their manuals. Units where you set up the MIDI mapping yourself get templates: Axe-Fx III, FM9, FM3, VP4, Boss GT-1000 and Darkglass Microtubes Infinity. Anything else, a synth, a looper: make your own tiles in a guided editor (Program Change, Control Change, bank select), add expression moves on any CC, add notes, and share the device as a file so others with the same gear just import it.

Amps & Modellers, Effects & Pedals

Your amp modeller on the first tab, a pedal on the second: the Whammy V / DT, Strymon (TimeLine, BigSky, Mobius, MX, Volante, V2...), Line 6 DL4 MkII and HX One, Boss DD / RV / MD-500, Meris, Chase Bliss, Walrus, Source Audio, EHX (beta), a template or your own MIDI device.

All 21 Whammy modes, plus DT Drop Tune

Whammy V or Whammy DT, laid out like the pedal's panel, engaged or bypassed, with heel-first switching. On the DT, Shift Up and Shift Down tiles drop or raise your tuning.

Treadle moves

Ramps, dives, trills and bends as tempo-synced CC#11 automation, or draw your own with the mouse (or generate a wave: sine, triangle, square, growing or speeding up, or import a move you drew in your DAW from a MIDI file) and save it in My drawings to reuse in any song.

Search & named clips

A forgiving search on every device finds a preset, performance or tile in a long list, even with a typo. Every drop is a clip called "Chorus" or "Whammy Dive 1 bar", so you read your show at a glance.

Guided setup

Each page shows its device's MIDI channel, a Test button and How to connect: a quick first-run tour and step-by-step DAW setup (Reaper as the example) for three ways of connecting your devices, with yours as the example. Note: the Quad Cortex's and the Kemper's MIDI Thru don't pass on USB MIDI, and the Whammy only has a 5-pin MIDI In, so it needs a MIDI path from an interface. One device on its own needs no chain at all.

A look inside

Drag any tile onto the arrangement. Click its play button to test it on the pedal first. Click a picture to enlarge it.

Install in two minutes

No code or build tools needed. Full details in the user guide.

Windows

VST3 + Standalone · Windows 10/11
  1. Download PedalCues-Windows-Setup.exe and run it.
  2. First time only: PedalCues isn't code-signed, so Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Click More info > Run anyway.
  3. Install: the VST3 goes to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\, the app to Program Files (Start menu: PedalCues). A newer installer replaces the old version.
  4. Your DAW: re-scan plug-ins (Reaper: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan). Prefer copying by hand? Get the zip (see the guide).

macOS

VST3 + AU + Standalone · Apple Silicon (macOS 11+) & Intel (10.13+)
  1. Download PedalCues-macOS.pkg and double-click it.
  2. First time only: PedalCues isn't notarised by Apple, so macOS says it can't verify the installer. Click Done, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
  3. Install: click Continue and Install. The app goes to Applications, the VST3 and AU plugins to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/.
  4. Your DAW: re-scan plug-ins (Reaper: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan). Had the zip version before? Delete the old copies in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/ so it isn't listed twice. Prefer copying by hand? Get the zip (see the guide).

Linux

VST3 + LV2 + Standalone · x86-64 · Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12, Fedora
  1. Download PedalCues-Linux.zip and unzip it.
  2. Install: run ./install.sh in the PedalCues-Linux folder: the VST3 to ~/.vst3/, the LV2 to ~/.lv2/, the app to ~/.local/bin/ and your applications menu. Run it again with a newer zip to update.
  3. Sync from QC over USB needs a one-time permission rule, then replug the QC: 
    echo 'KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="152a", TAG+="uaccess"' | sudo tee /etc/udev/rules.d/70-quad-cortex.rules && sudo udevadm control --reload-rules && sudo udevadm trigger

Support PedalCues

Hi, I'm Thanasis. My bandmate Leo and I play in ORIA, a progressive groove metal band from Thessaloniki, Greece. We were tired of programming MIDI by hand for every song, so I built PedalCues.

PedalCues is free and open source, and it will stay that way. If it saves you time at rehearsal or on stage, a coffee helps me keep improving it: new features, fixes, and support for more pedals. Completely optional. Thanks for playing loud! 🤘
