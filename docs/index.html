Everything a backing-track rig needs

Tested in Reaper; works in any DAW that can drag in MIDI clips and send a MIDI track to your pedals.

Presets & scenes

Your QC presets, scene names and colours as tiles. Sync your setlists, preset names, scene names and colours straight from the QC over USB. Scenes laid out like the pedal: A–D on top, E–H below. Scenes and stomps load their own preset first, or act on the one already loaded.

Stomps, tuner, gig view

Footswitch on/off, tuner for guitar changes, and Preset / Scene / Stomp mode, each one drag away.

All 21 Whammy V modes

Laid out like the pedal's panel, Classic or Chords, engaged or bypassed, with heel-first switching.

Treadle moves

Ramps, dives, trills and bends as tempo-synced CC#11 automation, or draw your own with the mouse.

Named MIDI clips

Every drop is a clip called "Chorus" or "Whammy Dive 1 bar", so you read your show at a glance.

Guided setup

A quick first-run tour and step-by-step Reaper setup for three ways of connecting your pedals. Note: the Quad Cortex's MIDI Thru doesn't pass on USB MIDI, so the Whammy needs a 5-pin MIDI path from an interface.

A look inside

Drag any tile onto the arrangement. Click its play button to test it on the pedal first. Click a picture to enlarge it.

Install in two minutes

No code or build tools needed. Full details in the user guide.

Windows

VST3 + Standalone · Windows 10/11
  1. Download PedalCues-Windows.zip and unzip it.
  2. Plugin: copy PedalCues.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  3. Standalone: run PedalCues.exe to test tiles without a DAW (pick the MIDI port in its MIDI Setup tab). If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info > Run anyway.
  4. Reaper: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan.

macOS

VST3 + AU + Standalone · Apple Silicon & Intel
  1. Download PedalCues-macOS.zip and unzip it. It isn't notarised by Apple, so clear the download flag once in Terminal (otherwise macOS says it "is damaged"): 
    xattr -cr ~/Downloads/PedalCues-macOS
  2. Plugins: copy PedalCues.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and PedalCues.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ (in Finder, Cmd+Shift+G opens a path)
  3. Standalone: drag PedalCues.app to Applications to test tiles without a DAW (pick the MIDI port in its MIDI Setup tab).
  4. Reaper: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan.

PedalCues is free and open source, and it will stay that way. If it saves you time at rehearsal or on stage, a coffee helps me keep improving it: new features, fixes, and support for more pedals. Completely optional. Thanks for playing loud! 🤘
